# A clause may record an exception with a scope

A more specific clause MAY declare `excepts:` on a general one, with a
`scope:` saying when the exception applies. The exception is recorded,
never inferred, and it MUST NOT target a `MUST` or `MUST_NOT` — a
repository that does not take the option still interoperates through
[[UZ-C-001]].
