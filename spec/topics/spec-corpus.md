# The uzushio specification corpus

How uzushio records its normative statements: clauses, the tests that enforce
them, the deviations and measurements taken of them, over the subjects they
speak to. Two clauses can only be seen to disagree where they are known to
be about one thing, and this is that thing for the shape of the vault itself.
