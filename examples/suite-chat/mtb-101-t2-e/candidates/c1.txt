Imagine you are participating in a race with a group of people. If you have just overtaken the last person, what's your current position? Where is the person you just overtook?

Then the answers would be:

My current position: Last (since I overtook the last person, I am now in last place)
The position of the person I just overtook: Second last (since they were previously last, but now I have overtaken them so they are second last)

So:

My current position: Last
The position of the person I just overtook: Second last
