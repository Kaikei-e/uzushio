## What was taken, and what was changed

Taken: the human `winner` verdicts of the `human` split, the answers the
compared models gave, and the questions they answered.

Changed: the pairwise verdicts were aggregated into three-way labels, and
the items were sampled. `DERIVATION.md` states exactly how, with the counts.
CC BY 4.0 asks that changes be indicated; that file is the indication.

Not changed: every `candidates/*.txt` file holds one model's answer as the
dataset carries it, byte for byte apart from a trailing newline and, on a
second-turn item, the two markers that join a model's two answers into the
transcript the annotator read. They are outputs of the models named in each
item's `gold.json`, produced in 2023, and this repository does not edit them —
a corpus whose answers have been tidied is not the corpus the human labels
were collected on.

## What is committed here, and what is not

Committed: `suite.json`, and per item `task.json`, `conversation.json`,
`gold.json` and `rubric.md`. Those are the prompts people wrote and the
judgements people made — the CC BY 4.0 part, redistributed under that licence
with this attribution.

**Not committed: `candidates/*.txt`.** Those are responses generated by the
models named in each `gold.json`, and a model's output is subject to its
provider's terms of use whatever licence the surrounding dataset carries.
This repository does not redistribute them. They are fetched from the source
onto the machine that needs them by

```sh
uzushio judge import-mtbench --candidates-only --out <this directory>
```

with the same `--seed` and `--target` the suite was derived at; `DERIVATION.md`
records the command. The fetch refuses to write unless the items it derives
are exactly the ones the manifest lists.
